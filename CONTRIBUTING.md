# Contribution Guideline for Ocean Apocalypse Projects
We would like to thank you for your interest in contributing to our open-source projects.
At Ocean Apocalypse, we're focused on FOSS-first software, but, as a small organization, it's hard to maintain all of our repositories. That way, we value contributions a lot.
Below, you'll find instructions on how to contribute, ensuring respect and effectiveness.

***

## Our expectations
We expect contributors to be respectful and civil at all times.

1. Contributions must respect the project's guidelines and scope.
2. Contributions must follow Ocean Apocalypse's [LLM Policy](#llm-policy).
3. Contributions must follow Ocean Apocalypse's [CI Policy](CI.md).
4. Contributions can be made via the use of code quality tools, as long as they don't violate the LLM Policy.
5. Pull requests, issues, documentation changes and feedback are all forms of contributions we accept.
6. Contributions must follow Ocean Apocalypse's [conventions](https://github.com/OceanApocalypse/conventions), if applicable.
7. Contributions should inform of licensing issues, if applicable.

### LLM Policy
At Ocean Apocalypse, we believe LLMs and generative AI harms FOSS more than it helps, as of right now.
That way, we discourage contributors from using LLMs and generative AI when contributing to our projects.
That being said, contributions with AI assistance must be fully disclosed and the contributor must review all of the AI's output, as well as show understanding of said output.
Under _those_ terms, the chance of the contribution being accepted is slightly greater.
Vibe-coded contributions won't be accepted under any terms.

For more information on why we have added this policy, click [here](https://codeberg.org/ethical-foss/open-slopware/src/branch/main/why_not_llms.md).

### Discarding contributions
When we discard a contribution, we always do our best to present a reason as to why it was discarded.
If you're not given any reason as to why your contributions was not accepted, it can be because the contribution breached an obvious rule in the contribution guideline (such as the LLM Policy or failing to be civil).
